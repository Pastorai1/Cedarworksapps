## Company summary  [slides 1–2]

Cedarworks Apps LLC is a Springfield, Missouri AI app studio that builds
focused, subscription AI apps for specific professions. Two apps are live today
on the Apple App Store, Google Play and the web: PastorAI ($47/month), an AI
ministry assistant for pastors and church staff, and Ready Room ($29/month or
$249/year), a voice AI sales-call simulator for sales reps. Our in-house app
factory, AppForge, finds proven app ideas, writes the code and builds the
marketing for each new app. Founded in 2026 by James Chambers (computer science
degree, 40+ years in sales), we are raising a $450,000 pre-seed round so the
founder can build full-time, market our two apps and take Cedarworks from 2
apps to 10.

## Problem you solve  [slide 3]

Pastors and sales professionals lose hours every week to repetitive prep and
unpracticed skills, and generic AI doesn't fit their work.

Pastors, often at small churches with little or no staff, write sermons, Bible
studies, devotionals, children's lessons and social media posts every week,
usually starting from a blank page. The load is heavy: 42% of U.S. pastors
considered quitting full-time ministry in the past year (Barna Group, 2022).

Sales reps learn discovery and objection-handling live, on real prospects, so
one fumbled objection can cost a deal worth thousands in commission. Structured
practice tools are mostly priced and sold for large enterprise teams, leaving
individual reps and small teams with nowhere to rehearse.

General chatbots like ChatGPT don't know these workflows or this vocabulary, so
people spend more time prompting than working. Our founder has seen both
problems first-hand: the weekly workload his bishop carries, and 40+ years in
sales watching reps learn on live deals.

## Your solution  [slides 4–14]

We build focused AI apps that each do one job really well, with no prompting
needed.

PastorAI ($47/month after a 14-day free trial) puts a pastor's whole week in
one dashboard: Sermon Builder, Series Planner, Bible Study guides, a week of
social media posts from one sermon, devotionals, children's lessons,
translation into 10 languages, and a sermon rehearsal tool, all written in the
pastor's own voice.

Ready Room ($29/month or $249/year) is a flight simulator for sales calls. Reps
sell out loud to a voice AI prospect that reacts to how they sell, choose from
18+ industry scenarios, and drill discovery and objections at easy, medium and
hard levels. They can also score their real calls from a live recording, an
upload or a transcript. An AI coach grades every session.

What makes us different is AppForge, our in-house app factory (internal only,
not sold). It works in four steps:
1. Know: the Brain stores what the AI should know about the owner and
   business in 7 areas (business, offers and products, audience, brand voice,
   story, goals, competitors). The Chief of Staff, an AI business partner
   grounded in the Brain, researches questions and helps decide what to do
   next.
2. Find: the Top 100 Apps Explorer ranks top-grossing apps by category and
   business model, and "Build a better version" starts a new app from a proven
   idea. App Types, Opportunities, Market Analysis and the Viability Scorer
   check demand, competition and fit before we build.
3. Build: Projects, Tech Stack and Build plan the app and write the code,
   reusing our proven AI, voice and billing setup. Store Listing writes the app
   store pages. Every app plugs into Business HQ, our shared operating system
   (dashboards, goals, key metrics, procedures, milestones).
4. Market: Attractive Character, Framework Extractor and the One-to-Many tools
   (emails, social posts, ads, presentations) build a cold-email and marketing
   funnel for each app, written in the owner's voice.

PastorAI and Ready Room were built before AppForge, and the lessons from
building them went into it. Apps 3–10 will be the first built with AppForge.

## Business model  [slides 18–19]

Monthly or annual SaaS subscriptions with a free trial first, sold through the
App Store, Google Play and the web: PastorAI $47/month after a 14-day free
trial; Ready Room $29/month or $249/year. More revenue will come from
multi-seat group plans for church staffs and sales teams, and custom AI app
builds for local businesses.

Unit economics (planning assumptions): after the 15% app-store fee and AI
costs we keep ~77% of PastorAI revenue and ~68% of Ready Room revenue. With an
average ~20-month subscription, lifetime value is ~$720 (PastorAI) and ~$390
(Ready Room). We'll spend at most a third of that to win each customer, which
pays back in about 7 months.

## Competitive landscape  [slide 21]

- Direct competitors: ministry software (e.g. Logos, Pulpit AI) and AI sales
  practice platforms (e.g. Second Nature, Hyperbound).
- Indirect competitors: general AI chatbots such as ChatGPT.

How we differ: general chatbots make users do the prompting and still give
generic results. Ministry tools focus on study, research or repurposing
content. Sales practice platforms are sold to enterprise sales teams through
a sales call. Cedarworks apps are built for one profession, ready to use with
no prompting, and self-serve at $29–$47 a month. AppForge lets us launch new
niche apps faster than larger competitors.
