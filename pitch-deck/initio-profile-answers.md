## Company description

Cedarworks Apps LLC is a Springfield, Missouri AI app studio that builds focused,
subscription AI apps for specific professions. Two apps are live on the Apple App
Store, Google Play and the web: PastorAI ($47/mo), an AI ministry assistant for
pastors and church staff, and Ready Room ($29/mo), a voice AI sales-call
simulator for sales reps. Our in-house engine, AppForge, analyzes the market to
choose and build our next eight apps. We are raising a $250K pre-seed round.

## Industry

Software / SaaS, and AI / Data. Consider removing Media & Gaming and
Marketplace, which don't describe what Cedarworks does.

## Problem statement  [slide 3]

Pastors and sales professionals lose hours every week to repetitive prep and
unpracticed skills. Pastors, often with little or no staff, write sermons, Bible
studies, devotionals, kids' lessons, social posts and translations from a blank
page. Sales reps learn discovery and objection-handling live, on real prospects,
so every fumbled objection costs a deal and a commission. Generic AI chatbots
don't know their workflow or vocabulary, so people spend more time prompting
than working. Our founder saw this first-hand: the weekly workload his bishop
carries, and 40+ years in sales watching reps learn on live deals.

## Solution & product  [slides 4–7]

Cedarworks builds focused AI apps that each do one job really well, with no
prompting needed.
- PastorAI ($47/mo, pastorai.io): sermon and series planning, Bible studies,
  devotionals, children's ministry lessons, church social media and
  translation, all written in the pastor's own voice.
- Ready Room ($29/mo, tryreadyroom.com): a "flight simulator" for sales calls.
  A voice AI plays a realistic, skeptical prospect. Reps practice discovery and
  objection-handling at three difficulty levels, and an AI coach grades each
  session.
- AppForge: our in-house engine that analyzes the market, selects the
  strongest app ideas and builds them on our shared stack, so each new app
  launches faster and cheaper.

## Business model  [slide 10]

Monthly SaaS subscriptions with free trials, sold through the App Store,
Google Play and the web: PastorAI $47/mo, Ready Room $29/mo. Additional
revenue will come from multi-seat plans for church staffs and sales teams, and
custom AI app builds for local businesses.

Estimated gross margin after the 15% app-store fee and AI costs: ~77%
(PastorAI) and ~68% (Ready Room). We target a lifetime value of at least 3x
customer acquisition cost, with about 7-month payback.
